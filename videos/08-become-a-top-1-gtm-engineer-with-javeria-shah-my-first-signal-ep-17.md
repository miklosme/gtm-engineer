What does the best GTM engineer actually look like? Not the one posting workflows on LinkedIn. The one quietly building systems that print pipeline month after month.

Javeria Shah won The Clay Cup, runs a two-person agency, and builds signal-based GTM engines for in-house teams and startups. In this episode, she breaks down her complete process — from the signal waterfall framework she uses to prioritize 10-20 signals in order, to why she reverse engineers signals by finding the signal first and the company second. She also gets into why the buy vs build debate is costing teams more time than they realize and how the 85/15 rule keeps her agency shipping results instead of chasing tools.

What you'll learn:

-The signal waterfall framework for prioritizing signals in order before falling back to generic outreach
- Why you need to use a reverse engineering approach to signals 
- Why referencing a weak signal is worse than no signal at all
- The buy vs build trap and how to decide when spending $500 on a tool beats a weekend of debugging
- How to create data packets for high-priority accounts instead of automating everything
- The 85/15 rule — 85% proven system, 15% experimentation
